I'm pleased to share that a new patent we have filed has recently been published and is now available through Google Scholar. In this patent, we present a novel method for reconstructing central pressure waveforms, including aortic and left ventricular, from peripheral measurements using machine learning techniques. This advancement allows for accurate continuous reconstructions of central pressures for bedside monitoring. 

You can view the published patent here: <a href = "https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250082280A1/en">[GOOGLE SCHOLAR]</a>
